## The problem

Odara's pitch starts from a gap. Over 70% of Nigerian healthcare is paid out of pocket, and hospitals wait 60–120 days for HMO reimbursement. The site had to explain four different credit products to four different audiences, and get each one to an eligibility check. The client asked for a full website.

## My role

I designed the landing page, the About and How It Works pages, a page for each of the four products (Pay, Equip, Receivables and Capital), and a short eligibility flow. The pages are built from a shared component set.
