## The brief

Vicelle Clothing is a fashion label that mixes minimal luxury with expressive design. The brand stands for confidence, originality and a clean look rooted in a modern African identity.

The label was brand new, with no logo or identity yet. The client asked for a logo and the branding around it: something with simple quality, that makes customers feel valued and seen.

## My role

I designed the identity and wrote it up as a 24-page brand guide: the logo and its variations, the rules for using it, the colour palette, the typography, a brand pattern, and mockups showing it in use.

## The logo

![Vicelle Clothing main logo: a navy wordmark with a turquoise and navy V above it](/images/vicelle/logo.webp)

A modern wordmark with a V that is half turquoise, half navy. The V also works alone as a small mark, for labels and profile pictures.

![The logo shown on white, navy, black and patterned backgrounds](/images/vicelle/backgrounds.webp)

The logo on light and dark backgrounds. The turquoise half of the V stays the same on every one.

![Logo construction lines showing the clear space around the logo](/images/vicelle/spacing.webp)

Clear space around the logo equals the height of the V. The smallest allowed width is 120px.

## Colour and type

![Colour palette: turquoise, white, black and deep navy](/images/vicelle/colours.webp)

Turquoise (#19C6C1) is the main colour. Deep navy (#1E2A38) grounds it, and black and white handle text and backgrounds.

![Proxima Nova type specimen in turquoise](/images/vicelle/typeface.webp)

Proxima Nova for headlines and key messages, Inter for body text, product descriptions and captions.

## The pattern

![A repeating pattern of turquoise and black slashes](/images/vicelle/pattern.webp)

The slashes come from the angle of the V. The pattern is for social post backgrounds, clothing tags, packaging and website banners.

## Key decisions

- **African fashion, without the usual African visual cues.** Vicelle makes African fashion, but I kept the identity away from an overtly African look: a clean wordmark, turquoise and navy, and a simple slash pattern.
- **Turquoise leads, navy grounds it.** Turquoise (#19C6C1) is the fresh, attention-getting colour. Deep navy (#1E2A38) sits next to it to add depth and keep the brand feeling calm and trustworthy. Black and white do the rest, so the palette stays simple.

## Outcome

The client approved the identity and is using it as they prepare to launch. The label is not public yet, so there are no results to report.
